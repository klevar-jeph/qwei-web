What Qwei does and doesn’t do

Does

  • Check every transfer on the server
  • Ask for your PIN or fingerprint before paying
  • Show the amount and recipient before you confirm
  • Log each naira in your activity feed in real time

Doesn’t

  • Charge anything when you tap an NFC request
  • Process payments on this website
  • Pay automatically from a link or QR scan
  • Claim features that aren’t built yet

Questions people ask

Is Qwei available to download?

The app works on Android today and the public release is coming soon. Until then, payment links show a preview page like the one above.

Can someone take money by sending me a link?

No. A link only opens the request. You see the details first and nothing is paid until you confirm inside the app with your PIN or fingerprint.

What if a payment request has expired?

The preview page tells you it has expired. Ask the sender for a new code or link.

Does this website handle my money?

Never. This site only explains Qwei and previews payment requests. Confirmations happen inside the app.
